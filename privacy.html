Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

At Meu Versículo, we value your privacy. This app was built to work without collecting personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, or store personal data on servers. We do not ask for sign-ups, contact information, location, or financial data, and we do not use analytics services or advertising.

Data stored only on your device

Some information is saved only on your device so the app can work, and it never leaves it:

  • your favorite verses and your journal of received verses;
  • the time you chose for the daily reminder;
  • usage counters for the free features.

When you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

Notifications

The daily reminder uses local notifications, scheduled on the device itself. No information is sent to servers for this.

Subscriptions and payments

Premium subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We have no access to your payment data. Managing and canceling subscriptions is done in your Apple account settings, and the handling of that data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share or sell any data to third parties — we have no data to share.

Your rights and contact

If you have any questions about this policy or about data protection laws (including Brazil's LGPD), reach out at vcsoares42@gmail.com.

By using Meu Versículo, you agree to this privacy policy.
